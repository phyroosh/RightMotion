# 🏷️ RightMotion Gen-Z Meme Reaction Stickers Board

Welcome to the **RightMotion Gen-Z Meme Reaction Sticker Board**.
This board catalogs 15 iconic internet reaction memes formatted as **tactile die-cut stickers** (`<MemeStickerOverlay />`).

---

## ⚡ Key Distinction: Opening Video Memes vs. Mid-Video Reaction Stickers

| Feature | Tactical Video Meme (`TacticalMemeCard`) | Meme Reaction Sticker (`MemeStickerOverlay`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Media Format** | MP4 Video (Muted, Fast-Forwarded) | High-Res PNG with White Die-Cut Border |
| **Placement** | **First-Frame Hook ONLY (`startFrame=0`)** | **Middle of the Video** (Beat 1, Beat 2, or Beat 3) |
| **Duration** | 40–46 frames (~1.3s–1.5s, hard cap 2.0s) | 24–36 frames (~0.8s–1.2s) |
| **Role** | Stop the scroll in the first 0.5 seconds | Relatable micro-reaction spike during spoken psychological insights |
| **Screen Space** | Full/dominant card behind collapsed transition | Tactile corner/side pop-in floating over the canvas |
